# TraffCOCO YOLO training

## Canonical layout

```text
TraffCOCO/
├── training/
│   ├── yolo26n.pt
│   ├── weights/
│   │   ├── best.pt
│   │   └── last.pt
│   ├── train.py
│   ├── inference.py
│   ├── validate.py
│   ├── dataset_utils.py
│   ├── config.yaml
│   └── ...
└── outputs/
    ├── YOLO_FORMAT/
    │   ├── images/
    │   ├── labels/
    │   └── data.yaml
    ├── training/
    │   ├── training_state.json
    │   ├── .training.lock
    │   └── train/
    │       ├── weights/
    │       │   ├── best.pt
    │       │   └── last.pt
    │       ├── results.csv
    │       ├── results.png
    │       ├── confusion_matrix.png
    │       └── ...
    └── inference/
        └── ...
```

Everything related to the YOLO training implementation and final model
files is kept under `training/`.

Everything generated by the training run is kept under `outputs/training/`.

The resumable training checkpoint is:
`outputs/training/train/weights/last.pt`

After successful training, final checkpoints are published to:
`training/weights/best.pt`
`training/weights/last.pt`

`inference.py` and `validate.py` load `training/weights/best.pt` by default.
Inference results are kept separately under `outputs/inference/`.
